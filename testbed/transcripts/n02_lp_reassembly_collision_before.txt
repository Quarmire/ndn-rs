Synthetic transcript: pre-fix, `ReassemblyBuffer::process` took a
4-arg signature `(seq, frag_index, frag_count, fragment)` and the
internal `pending` HashMap was keyed by `seq: u64` alone. The
regression test `n02_per_endpoint_keying_isolates_overlapping_sequences`
calls `process(endpoint_id, seq, ...)` with 5 args and asserts that
two endpoints with overlapping `seq=42` produce two distinct pending
entries — that fails to compile against the old signature
(E0061 "this method takes 4 arguments but 5 arguments were supplied")
and would otherwise observe `pending.len() == 1` because the second
peer's first fragment overwrites the first's slot.
